# Products and draft listings

```powershell
py digital.py --topic "Study habits" --audience "Adult learners" --days 14 --output .\drafts
```

Creates a workbook, CSV tracker, buyer readme, customer ZIP, SHA-256 manifest,
and internal Markdown/JSON draft listings. Output folders are never silently overwritten;
choose another --output folder when repeating a topic.
This is a deterministic template generator, not an LLM and not a market-research system.
It runs offline without AI usage fees. Topic and audience customize the prompts; a human
must edit and improve the product, check accuracy, rights, demand and marketplace policies,
choose price and publish manually. No marketplace integrations or automatic sales exist.
Marketplace transaction fees, taxes and optional services are outside this project.
No income or sales are promised. Customer ZIP intentionally excludes internal draft listings.
